How it works

Install → we do the boring, private, repetitive work. You keep the credits.

01
🔍

Find your traces

Scans every supported agent's local session directory and indexes the JSONL — no setup, no moving files.

02
⬆️

Upload — or auto-upload daily

Incremental sync daemon with sha256 dedup and an offline queue. Set it once, earn daily.

03
🖥️

View your trace

HF-style timeline viewer: prompts, tool calls, outputs, reasoning — fully searchable.

04
🪙

View your credits

A live ledger tracks every upload, view, and sale. Transparent, token-gated.

05
🔁

Exchange for USDC/USDT

Sell researcher/ commercial access on-chain. You set license and price per trace.
